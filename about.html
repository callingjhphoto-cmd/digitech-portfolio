About

The Person Behind the Lens

Not just another videographer. A drinks industry veteran who picked up a camera and never put it down.

My Story

James Huertas

I spent seventeen years inside the drinks industry before I ever picked up a professional camera. Bartending in London, brand ambassador work for LVMH, running events, building menus, training teams. I learned the language of spirits, wine, and hospitality from the inside out.

When I moved to Spain and started shooting, that industry knowledge became my unfair advantage. I don't just make things look good — I understand what brands are trying to say, who they're trying to reach, and what content will actually work for them. That's something you can't learn from a YouTube tutorial.

Today I work as a freelance videographer, photographer, and digital imaging technician (digitech) for production companies and brands across Spain and internationally. My home bases are Majorca and Barcelona, but I travel wherever the work takes me.

When I'm not behind a camera, you'll find me on my Yamaha Tenere 700, exploring the roads of Mallorca or planning the next adventure.
